## I got connected to Nishi-Chiba Station

At the beginning of this year, I walked around Nishi-Chiba Station in Chiba City, Chiba Prefecture, but I never imagined I would change jobs to a company in that location.  
You really never know what the future holds.

I had several job interviews, but they were all remote, so I wasn't very aware of where the company was located.

So when I got the job and looked up where the company was, it was near Nishi-Chiba Station, and I remembered filming a walking video there, so I watched the video again and remembered there were quite a few students walking around and there was a shopping street.
It's interesting how a place I happened to film became connected to me.
